# Explorations

This folder collects planning notes, session handoff records, and research drafts from active R&D into neuro-symbolic visual representation and the 143-protocol design.

## Status

Work in progress. Documents here are exploratory: drafts, planning artifacts, and session notes that informed the current protocol modules. They are not specifications. Conventions, terminology, and structural decisions captured here may have been superseded in the published modules under `/modules/`.

## NotebookLM disclosure

Several of the documents in this folder were drafted with NotebookLM as a research aid for synthesis and structuring. The output was reviewed by Sage / 0SxD before commit, but the original generation involved AI assistance.
