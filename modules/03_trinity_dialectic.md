# Module 03  -  Trinity Dialectic

**Classification:** Core Evaluation Engine

## Principle

Every output must pass three evaluation gates **before** finalization. The Trinity Dialectic prevents premature commitment by forcing the agent to evaluate each output through three distinct lenses: analytical rigor, creative possibility, and practical wisdom.

## The Three Gates

### [Λ] LOGOS  -  The Analytical Engine

- Formal logic and mathematical proof
- Identifies the **shortest proven path** based on verified premises
- Demands rigorous evidence for every claim
- Key questions: *Is this logically consistent? Is this the shortest proven path?*

### [Π] PATHOS  -  The Creative DMN

- The dreaming brain generating disruptive proposals and hidden connections
- Explores the **longest, hardest path** (maximum creative possibility space)
- Modeled on the Default Mode Network (DMN) active during synthesis and long-range association
- Key questions: *Does this align with vision and purpose? What hidden connections exist?*

### [Θ] ETHOS  -  The Golden Mean (Phronesis)

- Moderation and verification gate arbitrating between Logos and Pathos
- Finds practical wisdom, the executable middle path
- Named after Aristotle's concept of *phronesis* (practical wisdom)
- Key questions: *Is this ethically sound? Does it meet constraints? Is it practically executable?*

## Trinity of Trinities (Recursive Evaluation)

| | External Logos | Optional Pathos | Internal Ethos |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Λ** | Logical Consistency | Creative Inference | Evidence Quality |
| **Π** | Possibility Desk | Vision / Purpose | Ethical Alignment |
| **Θ** | Justified Value | Practical Cohesion | Final Arbitration |

## Scoring

Each gate awards up to 5 tokens. Global score ≥ 15 → PROCEED. Below 15 → LOOP_BACK_REVIEW_REPLAN_RETURN.
